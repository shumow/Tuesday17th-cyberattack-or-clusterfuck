# Provenance at a glance

[README](README.md) · **Provenance** · [Sources](sources.md) · [Timeline](timeline.md) · [Assessment](assessment.md) · [Comparison](comparison.md) · [Open questions](open-questions.md) · [Source check](source-check.md)

The main claims about NSANet in this repo, each traced to where it comes from and how good that source is. As of 17 November 2026.

**The pattern to notice:** the only primary material seen directly is 20 to 23 years old; everything technical since then comes through one hobbyist blog; and the current reporting is anonymous-source journalism plus one on-record agency statement, with no technical content.

## What was not done

The FOIA file, the 1998 thesis, the 2004 presentation and the Snowden TAO slide were not opened directly; they are known here only through the secondary sources that cite them and the pages that describe them. The Electrospaces page was first read in part; the [source check](source-check.md) read it in full, but could not open its job-posting and Quizlet footnotes.

Each of these is a task in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md#verification-to-do).
